Privacy policy.

Last updated 18 May 2026

This privacy policy (“Policy”) forms part of the User Agreements between you and Toreva Pty Ltd (“we”, “us”, or “the Company”). It describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information in connection with your use of the Service.

Privacy is core to how we build. The Service is non-custodial. We do not require your name, email address, phone number, or government-issued identification to use it, unless required by applicable law.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to all information collected through or in connection with the Service. It does not apply to third-party websites, protocols, or services that may be accessible through the Service. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

2. Information we collect

Because the Service is non-custodial, we collect as little as possible. This may include:

  • On-chain data: public wallet addresses and transactions you authorise, which are inherently public on the blockchain.
  • Usage data: limited, aggregated diagnostics to keep the Service reliable and secure.
  • Device data: basic technical information your browser or device sends when you connect.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate, secure, and improve the Service, to execute on-chain actions you authorise, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your information.

4. Your choices

You control your Wallet and your assets at all times. You can stop using the Service at any time, and you can manage device permissions through your browser or device settings.
